Resources

Helpful links

Waterloo Recreation Area

Michigan's largest state park in the Lower Peninsula, with trails, lakes and the Eddy Discovery Center, right next door.

Huron River Watershed Council

The nonprofit that protects the Huron River watershed, which Wild Goose Lake drains to.

Report a lake problem

Washtenaw County Water Resources Commissioner contacts for lake management, lake levels, flooding and illegal dumping.

County lake management

The County's lake improvement projects, including North Lake's, with reports, treatment information and natural shoreline resources.

Michigan Natural Shoreline Partnership

How to plant and keep a natural shoreline that protects the lake.

MapWashtenaw

The County's property map, for parcel lines, aerial photos and property records.

North Lake Protection Association

Our neighbor a few miles away, and the model for our lake improvement plan.

DNR waterfowl hunting

The Michigan DNR's hub for duck and goose hunters, with licenses, current rules, bird identification guides and hunting maps.

Michigan Duck Hunters Association

A nonprofit devoted to waterfowl and wetland conservation that coordinates Michigan's waterfowl stamp program with the DNR.
