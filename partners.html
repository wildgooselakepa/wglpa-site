State partners

The statewide groups we work with

Wild Goose Lake does not have to figure everything out alone. These Michigan organizations give us training, lake data, expert advice and a voice in Lansing.

Michigan Lakes and Streams Association

The statewide nonprofit for lake and stream associations. Membership for a group of up to 50 members includes The Michigan Riparian magazine, and MLSA hosts the annual Michigan Lakes and Streams Conference each spring, where volunteers train and pick up monitoring equipment.

mymlsa.org

MiCorps lake monitoring

The Cooperative Lakes Monitoring Program, run by the State with MLSA and Michigan State University, trains volunteers to test their own lake. Each test is a small yearly fee: water clarity, spring and summer phosphorus, and dissolved oxygen, with plant mapping available. Training is free, and the results go into a public state database.

micorps.net

Michigan Waterfront Alliance

A statewide advocacy group for lakefront owners and lake associations, supported entirely by membership dues. It speaks for riparian owners on state laws and rules that affect inland lakes.

michiganwaterfrontalliance.com

MiCorps enrollment opens each winter, with deadlines from March 1 to May 10.

Community partners

Foundations we plan to ask for help

Grants can carry the start-up work of a lake program, such as the first lake survey, monitoring equipment and volunteer training, so that less of it falls on owners. Once the association has its tax-exempt status, or a fiscal sponsor, we plan to approach these local foundations. None has made a grant to us yet.

5 Healthy Towns Foundation

Serves the Chelsea, Dexter, Grass Lake, Manchester and Stockbridge school districts, including Gregory. Its Stockbridge-Area Wellness Coalition funds local projects that help people move more and connect with each other. We plan to propose open-water swim and paddle mornings, a volunteer monitoring team and a centennial lake gathering.

5healthytowns.org

Community Foundation for Southeast Michigan

Makes community grants across seven counties, including Washtenaw, with a focus area for public space and the environment. We plan to ask about support for the baseline lake survey and invasive-plant control.

cfsem.org

Ann Arbor Area Community Foundation

Serves nonprofits across Washtenaw County. Wild Goose Lake was settled in 1927 by Black families from Ann Arbor, and we plan to ask how the Foundation might support a centennial project joining lake care, local history and help for neighbors in need.

aaacf.org

Chelsea Community Foundation

An affiliate of the Community Foundation for Southeast Michigan that supports the Chelsea community, including trails, parks and health and wellness. Many lake families shop, bank and see doctors in Chelsea, and we plan to ask whether a request from Wild Goose Lake can be considered.

chelseafoundation.org

Local businesses, churches, clubs and friends of the lake can be community partners too. A yearly gift to the Lake Program Fund helps keep the lake affordable for every owner. Make a gift or get in touch.
