Bylaws and Board slate: vote by December 12

The full Board slate and the proposed Bylaws will be posted on this website by November 14, 2026, for association members to review. Members then vote yes or no by Saturday, December 12, 2026. Nominations for every Board seat are open now; see the call for nominations. Owners who have not given us an email address will get a one-page paper notice. To receive notices by email, send your name, lake address and email to secretary.pa@wildgooselake.org.
