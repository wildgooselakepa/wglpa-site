About the association

Who we are

The Wild Goose Lake Protection Association (WGLPA) is a nonprofit organization of property owners and friends of Wild Goose Lake in Lyndon Township, Washtenaw County, about five miles north of Chelsea, Michigan, next to Waterloo State Recreation Area. Every owner of lakefront or lake-access property is a member, about 40 owners and 58 parcels in all. We are applying to the IRS for 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status.

Members pay $10 a year in dues for the association's running costs. Separately, gifts to our Lake Program Fund lower every owner's share of the cost of caring for the lake, and the Neighbor Fund helps households that still can't afford it.

Mission

To protect and improve the ecological health, recreational value and natural beauty of Wild Goose Lake for the people and wildlife who depend on it today and for generations to come.

Our goal is to catch problems early. We test the water, track changes from year to year, and act on what we learn, whether that means managing invasive plants, educating boaters or working with local and state agencies.
