Member ballot

Vote on the proposed Bylaws

Sign in, read the Bylaws, and vote yes or no by Saturday, December 12, 2026. Each property has one vote.

  1. Sign in
  2. Review the Bylaws
  3. Vote
Step 1

Sign in to vote

Use the email address the association has on file for you and the member password from your ballot notice.

Don't have the password, or your email isn't recognized? Write to secretary.pa@wildgooselake.org. You can also ask for a paper ballot.

Step 2

Review the proposed Bylaws

Read all six pages below before you vote. Open or download the PDF.

Step 3

Cast your ballot

Shall the members ratify the proposed Bylaws of Wild Goose Lake Protection Association, Inc.?

One vote per property. If you share the property with other owners, agree on your vote first. Once you submit, the vote can't be changed. The ballot is secret: the association records that your property voted, not how.

Ballot received

Thank you for voting

Your vote has been recorded. The results will be posted on this website after voting closes on December 12, 2026.
