Member ballot

Vote for the first Board of Directors

Sign in and vote for the first Board of Directors by Wednesday, December 23, 2026. Each property has one vote.

The proposed Bylaws are not part of this election. The membership will vote on the Bylaws in 2027, after they have been reviewed by legal counsel.

Voting opens on Tuesday, December 1, 2026.

  1. Sign in
  2. Vote
Step 1

Sign in to vote

Use the email address the association has on file for you and the member password from your ballot notice.

Don't have the password, or your email isn't recognized? Write to secretary.pa@wildgooselake.org. You can also ask for a paper ballot.

Step 2

Cast your ballot

Board officers

The first officers serve until the first annual meeting in September 2027.

Each property has one vote. If you own more than one property, your ballot counts once for each. If you share ownership, agree on your vote with your co-owners first. Only one of you can submit it. Once you submit, the vote can't be changed. The ballot is secret: the association records that your property voted, not how.

Ballot received

Thank you for voting

Your vote has been recorded. The results will be posted on this website after voting closes on December 23, 2026.
