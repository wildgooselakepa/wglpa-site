Ballot admin

For the Secretary. Load the voter list, follow turnout, record paper ballots and download the results. Results are not public until the Board posts them.

Sign in

The admin key is the ADMIN_TOKEN secret saved in Cloudflare. It is kept only in this browser tab and cleared when you close it.

Turnout and results

QuestionYesNoResult

Voting closes on its own at the end of December 12, 2026. Import the results file into Proton Sheets (File, Import) to keep a record.

Voter list

Keep the master list in Proton Sheets with the columns Email, Owner and Votes. When it changes, download it from Proton Sheets as CSV (File, Download, CSV) and upload it here. Uploading replaces the whole list. Votes already cast are kept.

Record a paper ballot

For owners who return a paper ballot. Use the owner name exactly as it appears on the voter list, so the same owner cannot also vote online. For owners not on the list, use the name on the tax roll and enter the number of Properties they own.

If the owner is on the voter list, the number of votes from the list is used.
