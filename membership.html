Membership

Everyone belongs.

Every owner of lakefront or lake-access property on Wild Goose Lake is a member.

If you own lakefront or lake-access property on Wild Goose Lake, you are a member of the association, with one vote per property at every meeting. Annual dues per household support the newsletter, the website, insurance, lake events and a modest reserve for the unexpected. The members can change the amount by vote, and no one loses membership or a vote over dues. Dues are separate from fundraising: our annual gifts to the Lake Program Fund and the Neighbor Fund are described under Fundraising.

Annual dues

Rate to be announced. We are preparing careful estimates of the association's first-year operating costs, and the dues will be set to match them. The rate and how to pay will be posted here once the budget is complete.

Not an owner? Friends of the lake, former residents and descendants of Wild Goose families are welcome as associate members and donors. Get in touch.
