We are neighbors who share a lake. Together we watch over its water, its shoreline and the wildlife that comes back every season.

The association

Who we are

A nonprofit of owners and friends of Wild Goose Lake in Lyndon Township, about five miles north of Chelsea, beside Waterloo State Recreation Area. Every lakefront and lake-access owner is a member.

About the association
Our mission

Protect and improve

The ecological health, recreational value and natural beauty of Wild Goose Lake, for the people and wildlife who depend on it today and for generations to come.

Our goal

Catch problems early

We test the water, track changes from year to year and act on what we learn, modeling our lake program on North Lake's.

Bylaws ratification meeting set for November 14

Every owner of lakefront and lake-access property is invited to vote on the association's Bylaws and elect the first Board of Directors on Saturday, November 14, 2026. The draft Bylaws, agenda and ballot will be posted on this website and emailed to every owner at least two weeks ahead; owners who have not given us an email address will get a one-page paper notice. To receive notices by email, send your name, lake address and email to secretary.pa@wildgooselake.org. Time and place
